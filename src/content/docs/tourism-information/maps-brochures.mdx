### **Official Resource Hub**


:::tip
For those who prefer to plan ahead or carry digital guides on their devices, the official tourism organizations of Hiroshima provide a comprehensive library of brochures. You can download a wide variety of PDF guides, including city maps, dining guides, and historical walking routes, directly from their integrated portal.
:::


#### Comprehensive Library


[bookmark](https://dive-hiroshima.com/en/information/post-26375/)


Access a one-stop page hosted by the Hiroshima Tourism Association where you can browse and download over 20+ different brochures in multiple languages.


#### Leaflets of the Hiroshima Peace Memorial Museum


[bookmark](https://hpmmuseum.jp/modules/info/index.php?action=PageView&page_id=48&lang=eng)


These documents include a floor map of the museum, summaries of the exhibits, and the historical significance of the Peace Memorial Park. Reviewing these in advance is highly recommended to better understand the profound message of the museum.
