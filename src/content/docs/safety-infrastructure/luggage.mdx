To make your visit to Hiroshima as comfortable as possible, we highly recommend utilizing "Hands-Free Travel" services. Whether you want to store your bags for a few hours or send them ahead to your next hotel, Hiroshima offers several convenient options.

### Luggage Storage


#### Coin Lockers at Hiroshima Station


Available in various sizes at the Shinkansen and North/South exits. Large lockers can accommodate full-sized suitcases.
