### How to Ride

- **Boarding**: You can enter through any door.
    - If you are paying with **Cash**, you do not need to touch or scan anything when entering.
    - **IC cards (Suica, PASMO, ICOCA, etc.) & WAON:** Tap your card or smartphone on the reader when entering (2-touch system).
    - _Note: People scanning their smartphones are using a specific local service._
- **Exiting & Payment:** Exit through the door where the driver or conductor is stationed.
    - **IC cards (Suica, etc.) & WAON:** You can exit through **any door** and tap your card/smartphone on the reader next to that door to pay.
    - **Cash / Day Pass:** Exit through the door where the driver or conductor is stationed, and pay or show your pass to them.

### Fares

- **Flat Rate:** Adults ¥240 / Children ¥120.
    - <u>Important: Japan Rail Pass (JR Pass) is NOT accepted.</u>

#### Payment Methods

- Cash
- IC Cards (Suica, PASMO, ICOCA, etc.)
- 1-Day Pass

### Tips

- **1-Day Streetcar & Ferry Pass (¥1,000):** If you are going to Miyajima, this pass is highly recommended as it covers the tram, the ferry, and the Miyajima Visitor Tax.
- _Note: This pass is valid ONLY for the Miyajima Matsudai Kisen ferry. It cannot be used for the JR Ferry._
