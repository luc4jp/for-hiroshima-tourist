### Etajima


Known for its historic Naval Academy and olive groves.

- **Route:** High-speed boats or ferries from **Hiroshima Port (Ujina)**.
- **Time:** Approx. 20–30 mins by high-speed boat.

### Okunoshima


World-famous as "Rabbit Island," home to hundreds of friendly wild rabbits.

- **Route:** Take a ferry from **Tadanoumi Port** (accessible via JR Takehara station).
- **Time:** Approx. 15 mins.

### Matsuyama


A major city on Shikoku island, famous for Dogo Onsen.

- **Route:** **Super Jet** (high-speed) or **Cruise Ferry** from **Hiroshima Port**.
- **Time:** 1 hr 10 mins (Super Jet) / 2 hrs 40 mins (Ferry).
