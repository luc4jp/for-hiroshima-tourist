### Routes & Stops



<BookmarkCard url="https://chugoku-jrbus.co.jp/sightseeing/files/20251024meipurupu_map_foreigner.pdf" title="chugoku-jrbus.co.jp" description="" />



All routes start and end at Hiroshima Station (Shinkansen Exit) and stop at the following major sites:

- Atomic Bomb Dome
- Peace Memorial Museum
- Shukkeien Garden


<BookmarkCard url="https://chugoku-jrbus.j-server.com/LUCCGKJB/ns/tl.cgi/https://chugoku-jrbus.co.jp/sightseeing/files/20250201meipurupu_schedule_en.pdf?SLANG=ja&TLANG=en&XMODE=0&XCHARSET=UTF-8&XJSID=0" title="chugoku-jrbus.j-server.com" description="" />



#### **Choose your route based on your destination:**

1. **Orange Route:** The only route that goes to the Hiroshima City Museum of Contemporary Art.
2. **Green Route:** Goes through the downtown areas like Shintenchi and Nagarekawa (great for shopping and dining).
3. **Lemon Route:** The fastest way to return to Hiroshima Station from the Peace Memorial Park area.

### How to Ride


#### Boarding:


Enter through the FRONT door (next to the driver).

- **Payment:** Pay your fare WHEN YOU BOARD.

#### Exiting:


Press the stop button and exit through the MIDDLE door.


### Fares

- **Single Ride:** Adults **¥240** / Children **¥120**
- **1-Day Pass:** Adults **¥600** / Children **¥300**
- **Japan Rail Pass:** **FREE**

#### Payment Methods

- **IC Cards:** Suica, PASMO, ICOCA, and other major transport IC cards are accepted.
- **Cash**
- **Japan Rail Pass (JR Pass):** Show it to the driver when boarding.
- **Day Passes**

#### Where to Buy Day Passes

- **On the Bus:** You can buy it directly from the driver.
- **Online:** Digital tickets are available via smartphone.
- **Ticket Counter:** Bus Ticket Counte**r** near the Hiroshima Station Shinkansen Exit (1st Floor).
    - **Hours:** 9:30 AM – 5:30 PM.
