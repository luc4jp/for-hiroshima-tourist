### Planning Your Visit


To see the main sites and the museum thoroughly, you should allow at least **3 to 4 hours**.

- **Opening Hours:** **The park itself is open 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.** You can visit at any time, even late at night or early in the morning.
- **Admission Fee:** **FREE.** There is no entrance fee to enter the park ground
    - **Note:** Only the **Peace Memorial Museum** requires an admission fee.

#### Peace Memorial Museum

### Peace Memorial Museum


#### Online Booking:



<BookmarkCard url="https://www.klook.com/en-US/activity/106669-hiroshima-peace-memorial-museum-permanent-exhibitions-admission/" title="klook.com" description="" />



**Essential:** Due to high demand, entry is managed by time slots. It is strongly recommended to book your tickets online in advance to ensure entry and skip the long lines at the ticket counter.


#### "Exclusive Entry" for Online Ticket Holders (Best for Avoiding Crowds):


:::tip
To avoid large crowds and see the exhibits in a quieter environment, we recommend visiting during these exclusive hours. These time slots are available ONLY for those who have purchased tickets online in advance.
:::

- **Dec – Feb:** 7:30 AM – 8:30 AM / 4:30 PM – 6:00 PM
- **Sep – Nov & Mar – Jul:** 7:30 AM – 8:30 AM / 5:30 PM – 7:00 PM
- **August:** 7:30 AM – 8:30 AM / 6:30 PM – 8:00 PM

#### Crowd Forecast & Peak Times:


![image](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1454923634634-bd1614719a7b?ixlib=rb-4.1.0&q=85&fm=jpg&crop=entropy&cs=srgb)


The museum can be extremely crowded depending on the time of day and season.


#### Busiest Seasons:

- **School Trip Seasons:** **Late May** and **mid-October to mid-November**. The museum will be packed with large groups of students.
- **Specific Dates:** **August 5th & 6th** (Peace Memorial Ceremony) and the **Obon holiday** (mid-August).
- **Holiday Periods:** **Cherry Blossom season** (late March to early April) and **Golden Week** (late April to early May).

#### Off-Peak Season:


**January & February:** Generally less crowded compared to the peak seasons, though there are still many visitors.


#### Peak Hours:

- 11:00 AM – 2:00 PM
    - _Tip: Aim for the "Exclusive Entry" hours (early morning or late afternoon) to avoid these peaks._
