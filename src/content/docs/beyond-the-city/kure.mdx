### **Kure: The City of Maritime History and Technology**


:::note
Located just 30 to 45 minutes from Hiroshima City, Kure is a historic naval port that played a pivotal role in Japan’s modernization. Surrounded by mountains and the sea, this former naval base is now a hub for world-class shipbuilding technology. It offers a unique perspective on Japan’s industrial heritage and maritime strength.
:::


### Key Landmarks


#### Yamato Museum



<BookmarkCard url="https://yamato-museum.com/leaflet/guidance-leaflet-english/" title="Guidance Leaflet English - 大和ミュージアム（呉市海事歴史科学館）" description="Guidance Leaflet English - 大和ミュージアム（呉市海事歴史科学館） - 巨大戦艦「大和」の残したメッセージ 『歴史を 未来へ』" />



Featuring a 1/10 scale model of the battleship Yamato, this museum chronicles Kure's history and the incredible engineering that led to Japan's post-war industrial success.


#### JMSDF Kure Museum (Iron Whale Museum)



<BookmarkCard url="https://www.jmsdf-kure-museum.go.jp/en/" title="jmsdf-kure-museum.go.jp" description="" />



This museum features a real 76-meter-long retired submarine. Visitors can go inside to see how crews lived and learn about minesweeping operations.


### Local Specialties


#### Maritime Curry (Kure Kaiji Curry)


Based on the secret recipes of the Japan Maritime Self-Defense Force ships. Each restaurant serves a unique version approved by the captain of a specific vessel.
