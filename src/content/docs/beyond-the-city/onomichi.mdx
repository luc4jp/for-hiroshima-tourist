### **Onomichi: The Nostalgic Town of Slopes and Seaways**


:::note
Nestled between steep hills and the Seto Inland Sea, Onomichi is a picturesque port town that feels frozen in time. Famous for its labyrinthine slopes, historic temples, and its role as the starting point of the Shimanami Kaido cycling route, Onomichi offers a poetic and peaceful escape. It has long been an inspiration for writers and filmmakers.
:::


### Key Landmarks & Experiences


#### Senko-ji Temple & Park


Perched on a hilltop, this iconic temple offers a breathtaking panoramic view of the town and the islands of the Seto Inland Sea. Visitors can take a ropeway to the top or walk up the charming, cat-filled alleys.


#### Shimanami Kaido


World-famous as a "cyclist’s sanctuary," this route connects Onomichi to Shikoku island via a series of majestic bridges across the sea.


### Local Specialties


#### Onomichi Ramen


A savory soy-sauce-based broth enriched with seafood dashi and topped with bits of back fat. It’s a local comfort food that is loved across Japan.
