:::note
Accessing Miyajima from central Hiroshima is simple and scenic. Most visitors travel via Miyajimaguchi, the gateway to the island, where you can catch a 10-minute ferry ride across the sea.
:::


### From Hiroshima Station to Miyajimaguchi


JR Route (Recommended for speed)

    - **Route:** Take the JR Sanyo Line from Hiroshima Station to **Miyajimaguchi Station**.
    - **Time/Cost:** Approx. 25-30 minutes / ¥420.
    - **Note:** If you have a **Japan Rail Pass**, this route and the JR Ferry are covered.

Hiroden Streetcar (For a local experience)

    - **Route:** Take Line #2 from central Hiroshima (Hatchobori/Kamiya-cho) to **Hiroden Miyajimaguchi**.
    - **Time/Cost:** Approx. 60-70 minutes / ¥270.
    - **Note:** It takes longer, but it's a great way to see the local neighborhoods and costs less.

### Ferry to the Island


Two Ferry Operators

    - **JR Miyajima Ferry:** Known for its "Great Torii Route," which sails closer to the Torii gate on trips heading to the island.

    ### Fares & Visitor Tax


    A "Miyajima Visitor Tax" (100 yen) is required in addition to the fare.

    - **One-way:** ¥300 (Fare ¥200 + Tax ¥100)
    - **Round trip:** ¥500 (Fare ¥400 + Tax ¥100)

    _Note: The Visitor Tax is collected only once per visit (usually included in the ticket price departing from Miyajimaguchi)._


    ### How to pay

    - **IC Cards (Suica, ICOCA, etc.):** You can tap at the gate. The tax is automatically added.
    - **Cash:** Buy a ticket at the vending machine. The tax is usually included in the price.

    |             | JR Miyajima Ferry                            | Miyajima Matsudai Kisen                    |
    | ----------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
    | Best For    | JR Pass holders & Great Torii views          | Streetcar users & Day Pass holders         |
    | Route       | Great Torii Route (sails close to the Torii) | Direct Route                               |
    | Passes      | Japan Rail Pass (Covered)                    | 1-Day Streetcar & Ferry Pass (Covered)     |
    | Credit Card | -                                            | Contactless Payment (Visa, etc.) supported |


#### Important Notes

- If using Japan Rail Pass:
    - The ferry fare is free, but the Visitor Tax is NOT included.
    - You must buy a "Visitor Tax Ticket" (¥100) at the vending machine before boarding.
- If using IC Cards (Suica, ICOCA, etc.):
    - The tax is automatically added when you tap the gate. (Total ¥300 for one-way).
- If using Credit Card (Matsudai Kisen only):
    - You can use Contactless Payment (Touch) with your Credit Card at the gate.

:::caution
Pass Restrictions: The 1,000-yen "1-Day Streetcar & Ferry Pass" is valid ONLY for Matsudai Kisen. The JR Pass is valid ONLY for JR Ferry.
:::


### **World Heritage Sea Route**


:::note
The "World Heritage Sea Route" is the most efficient and scenic way to travel between Hiroshima's two UNESCO World Heritage sites: the Atomic Bomb Dome and Itsukushima Shrine. This high-speed cruiser takes you from the Motoyasu River in the heart of the city directly to Miyajima Island in just 45 minutes, offering a unique perspective of Hiroshima from the water.
:::



<BookmarkCard url="https://www.aqua-net-h.co.jp/en/heritage/" title="aqua-net-h.co.jp" description="" />



#### Key Information

- **Departure Point:** Motoyasu-bashi Pier (located in Peace Memorial Park, near the Atomic Bomb Dome).
- **Time/Cost:** Approx. 45 minutes / Adult One-way ¥2,400 (Round trip ¥4,400).
    - _The Visitor Tax is collected only once per visit._
- **Reservation:** You can book online.

#### Why We Recommend This

1. **Seamless Connection:** No need to transfer between trains and ferries.
2. **Riverside Views:** Enjoy a relaxing cruise through the city's rivers before entering the open sea of the Seto Inland Sea.
